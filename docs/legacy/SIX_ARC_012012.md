# Every closed six-arc word 012012 costs more than the octagon

## Statement and inputs

Every strict-star admissible trajectory with six positive vertex-control
arcs **012012** has cost strictly greater than the smoothed octagon. All
positive durations and all initial shapes satisfying the actual endpoints
are included. The conclusion is invariant under the twisted rebasing and
cyclic relabeling of [VERTEX_WORD_REDUCTION.md](VERTEX_WORD_REDUCTION.md).

The argument constructs a **compact one-parameter family of actual
admissible trajectories**, with the given trajectory in its interior.
At both endpoints one arc vanishes and the result belongs to the already
compared five-arc orbit `01020`. Every member of the compact family remains
strict star. The forward four-arc constrained Hessian in
[FORWARD_ALTERNATION_EXCLUSION.md](FORWARD_ALTERNATION_EXCLUSION.md)
then proves that the cost minimum on this family occurs at an endpoint.
This supplies a full cost comparison, beyond the local nonminimality
conclusion of that reference.

No bound on arbitrary word length, comparison of the remaining six-arc
orbit, or reduction of general measurable controls is asserted. The proof
below consists of hand identities and ordinary real-variable arguments;
exploratory computations are not proof premises. No Lean source, build,
Lake command, or CI run is used.

## 1. Exact tangent endpoints and a positive three-parameter family

Use the rational coordinates and tangent-frame reduction of
[WORD_HOLONOMY.md](WORD_HOLONOMY.md), followed by the change of basis
\(L=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{smallmatrix}\right)\).
The tangent factors and endpoint matrix are

\[
T_0(q)=\begin{pmatrix}q&0\\0&q^{-1}\end{pmatrix},\quad
T_1(q)=\begin{pmatrix}q^{-1}&q-q^{-1}\\0&q\end{pmatrix},\quad
T_2(q)=\begin{pmatrix}q^{-1}&0\\q^{-1}-q&q\end{pmatrix},\quad
R'=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-1&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Both actual endpoints imply

\[
T_0(p)T_1(q)T_2(r)T_0(s)T_1(t)T_2(u)=R',
\qquad p,q,r,s,t,u>1. \tag{1}
\]

Write the first three-factor block as \(M=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{smallmatrix}\right)\).
Direct multiplication gives

\[
M=\begin{pmatrix}
\dfrac{p(1-(q^2-1)(r^2-1))}{qr}&\dfrac{pr(q^2-1)}q\\[3pt]
-\dfrac{q(r^2-1)}{pr}&\dfrac{qr}p
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The second block is necessarily

\[
M^{-1}R'=\begin{pmatrix}b&d-b\\-a&a-c\end{pmatrix}. \tag{2}
\]

For a block \(T_0(P)T_1(Q)T_2(U)=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{smallmatrix}\right)\),
the positive factors are recovered by

\[
P^2=\frac{a+b}{c+d},\qquad Q^2=1+b(c+d),\qquad U^2=\frac d{c+d}.
\]

Thus (1) has precisely the positive-factor domain

\[
p,q,r>1,\quad (p^2-1)(q^2-1)<1,\quad(q^2-1)(r^2-1)<1,
\]

and its remaining factors are

\[
s=\frac r{\sqrt{r^2-1}},\quad
t^2=r^2-q^2(r^2-1)\frac{p^2-1}{p^2},\quad
u=\frac{pt}{q\sqrt{r^2-1}}. \tag{3}
\]

Indeed the two extra inequalities are exactly \(d>b\) and \(a>0\)
in (2); every other positive-factor condition follows from the first
block. This derivation uses the full tangent matrix, not just its trace.

The following coordinates make a compact deformation explicit. Fix

\[
r>1,\qquad h=\sqrt{r^2-1},\qquad z=r-h\in(0,1),
\qquad z<v<1.
\]

Put

\[
e=1-v,\quad B=2h-e,\quad C=2-e,\quad
c_* =\frac{e^2}{2(h-e)}.
\]

Here \(h>1-z>e>0\). For \(0\le c\le c_*\), define

\[
x=e+2c,\qquad y=\frac{e-Bc}{v-c}. \tag{4}
\]

The six factors are equivalently

\[
\boxed{
p=\frac{x+B}{x+y},\quad q=\frac{x+B}{2h}=1+\frac ch,
\quad r=r,\quad s=\frac rh,
\quad t=\frac{y+C}{2},\quad u=\frac{y+C}{x+y}.}
\tag{5}
\]

For completeness, this is an exhaustive change of coordinates in the
open domain above. Starting from (3), set

\[
c=h(q-1),\quad A=1-h^2(q^2-1)>0,\quad
w=t-\frac{qh}{p}>0,\quad v=w+c.
\]

Then

\[
p=\frac{2qh\,w}{A-w^2},\quad
t=\frac{A+w^2}{2w},\quad u=\frac{A+w^2}{A-w^2}.
\]

The condition \(p>1\) becomes \(v>z\). Moreover
\(0<w<\sqrt A<1\), so \(t>1\) forces
\(w<1-\sqrt{1-A}\), hence \(v<1\). On this branch \(t>1\)
is exactly \(c<c_*\). Conversely, \(z<v<1\) and \(0<c<c_*\) give (5)
and all of (3). One can check the substitution without division by a
possibly vanishing expression:

\[
A-w^2=w(x+y)>0,\qquad A+w^2=w(C+y),\qquad w=v-c>0.
\]

Indeed \(c_*<z<v\): as a function of \(v\), \(c_*\) decreases
strictly from \(c_*(z)=z\). All denominators in these formulas are
therefore positive, including at the compact family's endpoints.

The identity

\[
\boxed{(C-x)(B-y)=2(v^2+2hv-1)>0} \tag{6}
\]

will also be useful. Throughout the closed interval,

\[
e\le x\le\frac{eh}{h-e}<C,\qquad
e\le y\le\frac ev<B. \tag{7}
\]

The inequalities are strict at the lower ends in the interior. Here
\(v^2+2hv-1>0\) follows from \(v>z\) and \(z^2+2hz=1\).
It proves \(B>e/v\) and \(C>x\), and differentiation of (4) gives
\(y'=-(v^2+2hv-1)/(v-c)^2<0\). At \(c=0\), only \(q\) equals
one; at \(c=c_*\), only \(t\) equals one. The other five factors
are strictly greater than one at either endpoint.

## 2. The actual initial state and all six active margins

The state half-factors are \(H_i(Q)=T_i(Q^{-1/2})\). Write

\[
\eta=\frac{1-z}{h}=\frac{2z}{1+z}>0,\quad
d_0=\eta e,\quad N=d_0(4-d_0)>0,\quad
D=\frac N{\eta^2}=e\left(C+\frac2z\right).
\]

The total state conjugator is
\(H=H_2(u)H_1(t)H_0(s)H_2(r)H_1(q)H_0(p)\), and the state
endpoint says that \(K=R'H\) commutes with \(X_0\). Substitution
of (5) gives the short exact matrix

\[
\boxed{K=\begin{pmatrix}
1-\eta(e+c)&\dfrac{2e+2\eta c(e+c)}{x+y}\\[4pt]
-\dfrac{\eta(x+y)}2&1+\eta c
\end{pmatrix}.} \tag{8}
\]

One way to verify the product is to use

\[
H_0(Q)=Q^{-1/2}\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&Q\end{pmatrix},\quad
H_1(Q)=Q^{-1/2}\begin{pmatrix}Q&1-Q\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\quad
H_2(Q)=Q^{-1/2}\begin{pmatrix}Q&0\\Q-1&1\end{pmatrix},
\]

and \(\sqrt{pqrstu}=qru\), which follows from (3). The lower-left,
lower-right, and upper-left entries of the product are respectively
\(-\eta(x+y)/2\), \(1+\eta c\), and \(1-\eta(e+c)\);
determinant one gives the remaining entry in (8). Its determinant can
also be checked directly from the displayed matrix.

In particular, \(\operatorname{tr}K=2-d_0\). The matrix is noncentral
and elliptic, and its upper-right entry is positive. The only traceless
determinant-one possibilities commuting with it are

\[
X_0=\pm\frac{2K-\operatorname{tr}(K)I}{\sqrt N}.
\]

The plus sign is selected by strict star membership. The verification of
positivity below also constructs this branch at every point of the compact
family. No choice of initial shape remains free.

Remove the common factor \(2\eta/\sqrt N\) from the three margins and
call the resulting quantities raw margins. Their invariant is
\(r_0r_1+r_1r_2+r_2r_0=D\). Set

\[
E_2=e\frac{1+z}{1-z},\qquad E_3=\frac ez,\qquad
W(a,b)=\frac{D-ab}{a+b}.
\]

The complete switching-state table is

| Stage | Raw margins | Next active raw margin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Initial | \((x,y,W(x,y))\) | \(x\) |
| After 0, factor \(p\) | \((x,B,W(x,B))\) | \(B\) |
| After 1, factor \(q\) | \((W(B,E_2),B,E_2)\) | \(E_2\) |
| After 2, factor \(r\) | \((E_3,W(E_2,E_3),E_2)\) | \(E_3\) |
| After 0, factor \(s\) | \((E_3,C,W(E_3,C))\) | \(C\) |
| After 1, factor \(t\) | \((W(C,y),C,y)\) | \(y\) |
| After 2, factor \(u\) | \((W(x,y),x,y)\) | — |

For the two fixed middle arcs, the needed factor identities are

\[
r=\frac{(E_2+E_3)(B+E_2)}{E_2^2+D},\qquad
s=\frac{(E_3+C)(E_2+E_3)}{E_3^2+D}.
\]

They follow by substituting \(h=(1-z^2)/(2z)\) and
\(r=(1+z^2)/(2z)\). For example
\(E_2+E_3=2er/(1-z)\); this cancels the first denominator, and
the second ratio reduces to \(2rz/(1-z^2)=r/h\). The other four
rows follow immediately from (5), the invariant, and

\[
B^2+D=2h(B+E_2),\qquad C^2+D=2(C+E_3).
\]

The final row is exactly the required terminal permutation of the initial
state. Together with the tangent product, it also implies the full frame
endpoint, by the tangent-frame argument in WORD_HOLONOMY.

## 3. Strict star membership on the whole compact family

All six active raw margins \(x,B,E_2,E_3,C,y\) are positive. It remains
to check that every adjacent product in the table is strictly less than
\(D\). These checks include the two arc-vanishing endpoints.

First, by (7),

\[
D-Bx\ge e\left[C+\frac2z-
\frac{h(2h-e)}{h-e}\right]>0. \tag{9}
\]

For fixed \(z,h\), the bracket is strictly decreasing in \(e\), since
its derivative is \(-1-h^2/(h-e)^2\). At \(e=1-z\) its value is
\(z+z^{-1}>0\); our domain has \(e<1-z\). Since \(y<B\), the
same estimate gives \(xy<Bx<D\).

The other four adjacent products obey

\[
D-BE_2=e\left[2h+e\left(\frac{1+z}{1-z}-1\right)\right]>0,
\]

\[
\frac z e(D-E_2E_3)
=2(1+z)-e\left(\frac{1+z}{1-z}+z\right)>0,
\]

\[
D-E_3C=e\left(C+\frac ez\right)>0,
\]

\[
D-Cy\ge e\left[\frac2z-\left(\frac1v-v\right)\right]>0.
\tag{10}
\]

For the second inequality use \(e<1-z\), making its subtracted term
less than \(1+2z-z^2<2(1+z)\). For the last, \(v>z\) gives
\(v^{-1}-v<z^{-1}\). Thus every \(W\) in the table is positive.
On a vertex arc one margin is constant, one increases, and one decreases;
positive values at both endpoints imply positivity throughout the arc.

We have therefore constructed actual strict-star integral trajectories
for every \(c\in[0,c_*]\), with a zero-duration arc deleted at an
endpoint. This is not a deformation that exits the admissible domain
before reaching its boundary. The formulas and the exact vertex cost
are continuous on this compact interval and smooth in its interior.

## 4. After rebasing, this is the exact forward-edge endpoint fiber

Rebase at the start of the third original arc, whose label is 2. The
twisted cyclic word becomes

\[
2,0,1,2,1,2,
\]

with respective factors \(r,s,t,u,p,q\). At the new initial point the
state is the third row of the table, depending only on the fixed
\(r,v\), not on \(c\). Normalize the new initial frame to the identity.
The first two arcs, with factors \(r,s\), have fixed active margins,
fixed durations, and fixed states and frames.

The remaining window has precisely the forward word **1212**. Its
initial frame and state are fixed by those two arcs. Its terminal frame
is \(R\), and its terminal state is the fixed conjugate of the new
initial state. Thus both local endpoints \((g,X)\) are fixed exactly
throughout this deformation. Free terminal time is allowed. This is the
edge endpoint fiber in FORWARD_ALTERNATION_EXCLUSION, not merely a
deformation preserving one scalar holonomy invariant.

The parameter is nondegenerate. The last arc has factor
\(q=1+c/h\) and fixed positive active margin
\(2\eta B/\sqrt N\); its duration is

\[
\tau_q(c)=\frac{\eta B}{\sqrt N}\log(1+c/h),\qquad
\tau_q'(c)>0.
\]

Its terminal state is fixed. Since the edge coordinate increases with
strictly positive speed, backward constant-control flow from that state
makes the last switch move with nonzero derivative. Hence the resulting
curve of three edge-switch coordinates has a nonzero tangent. The
endpoint map has rank two, as proved in the referenced four-arc theorem.

## 5. Compact minimization and comparison of both boundary trajectories

Let \(J(c)\) be the actual cost of the rebased trajectory. It is
continuous on \([0,c_*]\), and therefore attains its minimum. It has
no interior local minimum. Indeed, if its derivative is nonzero, the
parameter direction already decreases cost. At an interior stationary
point, the forward-edge constrained Hessian is strictly negative on
every nonzero tangent to the exact endpoint fiber. Section 4 supplies
such a tangent. Since that fiber is one-dimensional, \(J'(c)=0\)
is stationarity on the whole fiber, not just on a smaller subspace.
Thus \(J''(c)<0\), again ruling out a local minimum.

Consequently, for every \(0<c<c_*\),

\[
\boxed{J(c)>\min\{J(0),J(c_*)\}.} \tag{11}
\]

At \(c=0\), the last factor \(q\) of the rebased word is one;
deleting it leaves `20121`, or `01202` after cyclic relabeling.
At \(c=c_*\), the factor \(t\) is one; deleting it leaves
`20212`, or `01020` after cyclic relabeling. Both are in the complete
noncyclic five-arc orbit proved in
[FIVE_ARC_PROGRESS.md](FIVE_ARC_PROGRESS.md). All five remaining
durations are positive, and Section 3 verified their strict star
conditions and full endpoints. Their costs are therefore each strictly
greater than the octagon's cost. Equation (11) proves the claimed strict
comparison for the entire six-arc family.

The compact family and its strict star bounds are essential here: the
local four-arc improvement theorem alone does not give a global cost
bound. Nor has this argument proved a six-to-five replacement theorem
for an arbitrary local six-arc window with arbitrary fixed endpoint
data. It treats the full closed admissible word (1).
