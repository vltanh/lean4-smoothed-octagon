# Algebra for the central upper duration faces

This is the complete algebra appendix for
[the upper duration proof](SHORT_DURATION_CENTRAL_UPPER_FACES.md).
Its indices are `j=0,1,2,3` for the four ratio intervals and
`f=1,8` for the two duration tests. All identities use those notes'
exact definitions of `N`, `X`, and `A`; partial derivatives are taken
before the coordinate substitution.

## 1. Second derivative identities

In each display the right side is `partial_x^2 N_{j,f}/2`.
Its coefficients in `X` are grouped as polynomials in `A` only to
make the elementary negative-term bounds easy to check. These are the
actual polynomials, not a list of coefficient norms.
