## Expansion audit

Every expanded identity in this appendix has at most 41 monomials and total degree at most eight in at most two variables. The largest displayed polynomial coefficient has 12 decimal digits. The definition of each numerator is kept as a product expression until differentiation, restriction to an edge, or removal of the positive factor `3a²` has reduced its degree. No expanded degree-ten numerator or large coefficient table is needed. Exact symbolic multiplication checked the printed identities as a transcription check; all signs follow from the elementary comparisons in the main proof.
