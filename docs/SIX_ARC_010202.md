# Every positive six-arc word 010202 costs more than the octagon

## Statement and scope

Every actual admissible trajectory with the six positive-duration vertex
pieces **010202** has cost strictly greater than the smoothed octagon.
The factors, durations, and initial state may be asymmetric. The same
conclusion holds after the cost-preserving twisted rebasing and cyclic
relabeling described in [VERTEX_WORD_REDUCTION.md](VERTEX_WORD_REDUCTION.md).

The proof imposes both actual endpoint conditions, determines the initial
state, computes the exact cost, and bounds that cost on a larger explicit
parameter domain. It uses the sharp scalar envelope proved in
[FIVE_ARC_PROGRESS.md, Sections 5–6](FIVE_ARC_PROGRESS.md). No Pontryagin
condition or strong transversality condition is assumed. In particular,
the conclusion is a cost comparison for every admissible member of this
word class, not only an exclusion of a proposed local minimum.

This does not compare the other six-arc classes or arbitrary longer words.
All arguments below are hand calculations. No numerical or symbolic
computation, Lean compilation, or kernel validation is used in this note.

## 1. Both endpoints and the tangent product

Use the rational coordinates of [WORD_HOLONOMY.md](WORD_HOLONOMY.md),
then conjugate by \(L=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{smallmatrix}\right)\),
as in [FIVE_ARC_PROGRESS.md](FIVE_ARC_PROGRESS.md). This diagonalizes the
label-0 tangent factor. In this basis,

\[
R'=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-1&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
T_0(q)=\begin{pmatrix}q&0\\0&q^{-1}\end{pmatrix},
\]
\[
T_1(q)=\begin{pmatrix}q^{-1}&q-q^{-1}\\0&q\end{pmatrix},\qquad
T_2(q)=\begin{pmatrix}q^{-1}&0\\q^{-1}-q&q\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{1}
\]

For an arc with active margin \(a>0\) and duration \(\tau>0\), its factor
is \(q=e^{2\tau/a}>1\). The tangent frame \(V=gX\) satisfies the actual
constant-control equation, so both admissible endpoints imply

\[
T_0(p)T_1(q)T_0(r)T_2(s)T_0(t)T_2(z)=R',\qquad
p,q,r,s,t,z>1.
\tag{2}
\]

The first three factors form an upper triangular matrix and the last
three form a lower triangular matrix. Write them as

\[
\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\0&a^{-1}\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\begin{pmatrix}c&0\\e&c^{-1}\end{pmatrix},
\]

where

\[
a=\frac{pr}{q},\quad b=\frac{p(q-q^{-1})}{r},\quad
c=\frac{t}{sz},\quad
e=(s^{-1}-s)\frac{t}{z}+\frac{s}{t}(z^{-1}-z).
\tag{3}
\]

Their product equals \(R'\) exactly when
\(ac=1\), \(b=c\), and \(e=-a\); the upper-left entry then vanishes
automatically. The equation \(ab=1\) gives
\(p=q/\sqrt{q^2-1}\). Substituting \(c=1/a\), equivalently
\(t=sz/a\), into \(e=-a\) gives
\(a^2=z^2(s^2-1)\). All factors are positive. Thus (2) is equivalent to

\[
\boxed{
p=\frac{q}{\sqrt{q^2-1}},\qquad
t=\frac{s}{\sqrt{s^2-1}},\qquad
r=z\sqrt{(q^2-1)(s^2-1)}.}
\tag{4}
\]

Introduce parameters

\[
u=\sqrt{\frac{q-1}{q+1}},\qquad
v=\sqrt{\frac{s-1}{s+1}},\qquad 0<u,v<1,
\]
\[
e_0=(1-u)(1-v),\qquad
f_0=\frac{4uv}{(1+u)(1+v)},\qquad d=\frac{e_0}{z}>0,
\]

and scalar functions

\[
P(w)=\frac{1+w^2}{2w},\qquad Q(w)=\frac{1+w^2}{1-w^2}.
\]

Then the complete positive-factor tangent family is

\[
\boxed{
(p,q,r,s,t,z)
=\left(P(u),Q(u),\frac{f_0}{d},Q(v),P(v),\frac{e_0}{d}\right),
\qquad 0<d<\min(e_0,f_0).}
\tag{5}
\]

No initial-state condition has been used in obtaining this family. In
particular, both strict inequalities on \(d\) have a direct meaning:
they are precisely positivity of the third and sixth arc durations.

## 2. State holonomy and the unique initial shape

The state transition on an arc is conjugation by
\(H_i(q)=T_i(q^{-1/2})\). Its total conjugator has the reverse order

\[
H=H_2(z)H_0(t)H_2(s)H_0(r)H_1(q)H_0(p).
\]

For a check without a long expansion, the two three-factor products are

\[
H_0(r)H_1(q)H_0(p)
=\frac1{\sqrt{pqr}}
\begin{pmatrix}q&p(1-q)\\0&pr\end{pmatrix},
\]
\[
H_2(z)H_0(t)H_2(s)
=\frac1{\sqrt{stz}}
\begin{pmatrix}zs&0\\s(z-1)+t(s-1)&t\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{6}
\]

Equation (4) gives \(\sqrt{pqrstz}=qsz\). Their product therefore is

\[
H=\begin{pmatrix}1&-u\\h&1-uh\end{pmatrix},\qquad
h=1-\frac{1-v}{z}=1-\frac{d}{1-u}.
\tag{7}
\]

Since \(d<e_0\), one has \(v<h<1\). Set

\[
N=d(4-d)>0,\qquad K=R'H.
\]

The terminal state condition is \([K,X_0]=0\). Here
\(\det K=1\), \(\operatorname{tr}K=2-d\), and the upper-right entry
\(1-uh\) is positive. Thus \(K\) is noncentral and elliptic. The
centralizer argument in [WORD_HOLONOMY.md, Section 4](WORD_HOLONOMY.md)
gives precisely two traceless determinant-one candidates,

\[
X_0=\pm\frac{2K-\operatorname{tr}(K)I}{\sqrt N}.
\tag{8}
\]

The plus sign has the positive margins

\[
(p_0,p_1,p_2)=\frac2{\sqrt N}(A_u,d,B_u),\qquad
A_w=d\frac{1+w}{1-w},\quad B_w=2(1-w)-d.
\tag{9}
\]

For example \(A_u=(1+u)(1-h)\) and
\(B_u=(1-u)(1+h)\). The opposite sign has all three margins negative.
Actual strict star membership therefore selects exactly (9).

To check the margin convention directly, the matrices defining
\(p_i(Y)=\operatorname{tr}(D_iY)\) in this basis are

\[
D_0=\begin{pmatrix}-1&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\quad
D_1=\begin{pmatrix}1&-2\\0&-1\end{pmatrix},\quad
D_2=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\2&-1\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Applied to \(2K-\operatorname{tr}(K)I\), their traces are
\(2A_u,2d,2B_u\), respectively.

For the remainder of the calculation call the quantities inside the common
factor \(2/\sqrt N\) the raw margins. They obey the exact invariant

\[
r_0r_1+r_1r_2+r_2r_0=N.
\tag{10}
\]

## 3. Every intermediate state and active margin

For \(0<w<1\), define

\[
K_w=\frac{4w}{1+w}-d,\qquad
L_w=\frac{4w(1-w)-d(1+2w-w^2)}{1+w^2}.
\tag{11}
\]

The exact vertex recurrence from [WORD_HOLONOMY.md, Section 5](WORD_HOLONOMY.md)
gives the following table.

| Stage | Raw margins \((r_0,r_1,r_2)\) | Next active raw margin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Initial | \((A_u,d,B_u)\) | \(A_u\) |
| After 0, factor \(P(u)\) | \((A_u,d/u,L_u)\) | \(d/u\) |
| After 1, factor \(Q(u)\) | \((d,d/u,K_u)\) | \(d\) |
| After 0, factor \(f_0/d\) | \((d,K_v,d/v)\) | \(d/v\) |
| After 2, factor \(Q(v)\) | \((A_v,L_v,d/v)\) | \(A_v\) |
| After 0, factor \(P(v)\) | \((A_v,B_v,d)\) | \(d\) |
| After 2, factor \(e_0/d\) | \((B_u,A_u,d)\) | — |

The final row is the required cyclic permutation of the initial margins.
Here are short checks of the entries that depend on the extra arcs:

\[
\begin{aligned}
(d+d/u)\frac{f_0}{d}-d&=K_v,&
(d+K_u)\frac{d}{f_0}-d&=d/v,\\
(d+A_v)\frac{e_0}{d}-d&=B_u,&
(d+B_v)\frac{d}{e_0}-d&=A_u.
\end{aligned}
\tag{12}
\]

The entries involving \(L_w\) can also be checked from the invariant:

\[
(A_w+d/w)L_w=N-A_wd/w,\qquad
(d+d/w)K_w=N-d^2/w.
\tag{13}
\]

An actual admissible trajectory has all these raw margins positive.
The comparison below holds on the entire domain (5), so no claim that
tangent closure alone implies all intermediate star inequalities is
needed. Restricting to the actual feasible subset only strengthens the
scope of the comparison.

## 4. Exact normalized cost

Let \(\delta=\operatorname{cost}/\sqrt{12}\). If the active raw margin is
\(a\), the growing and decreasing raw margins are \(b,c\), and the factor
is \(q\), exact integration of the vertex recurrence gives

\[
N\delta_{\rm arc}
=\frac a2\bigl((a+b)(q-1)+(a+c)(1-q^{-1})\bigr)
-\frac{a^2}2\log q.
\tag{14}
\]

The nonlogarithmic bracket is
\(a[(b_{\rm new}-b)+(c-c_{\rm new})]\). Its sum over the six rows can
be grouped into two expressions, one for \(w=u\) and one for \(w=v\):

\[
\begin{aligned}
E_w={}&A_w(d/w-d+B_w-L_w)\\
&+(d/w)(K_w-L_w+A_w-d)\\
&+d(K_w-d/w+B_w-A_w).
\end{aligned}
\tag{15}
\]

This grouping includes the two active-\(d\) arcs once each; it is just
the sum from the table, not an additional symmetry assumption. Put
\(\alpha=(1+w)/(1-w)\), so \(A_w=d\alpha\). Eliminating \(L_w\)
by (13) gives

\[
\frac{E_w}{d}
=B_w(\alpha+1)+K_w(1+1/w)-\frac Nd
 +\frac{3d\alpha}{w}-2d\alpha-\frac{2d}{w}.
\]

Now \(\alpha+1=2/(1-w)\), \(K_w=4w/(1+w)-d\),
\(B_w=2(1-w)-d\), and \(N/d=4-d\). Substitution reduces the
last display to \(4+2d\). Therefore

\[
\frac{E_u+E_v}{2}=2d(d+2).
\tag{16}
\]

The six active margins in the table now give

\[
\begin{aligned}
N\delta={}&2d(d+2)-\frac{d^2}{2}\left[
\left(\frac{1+u}{1-u}\right)^2\log P(u)
 +u^{-2}\log Q(u)+\log(f_0/d)\right.\\
&\left.\hspace{32mm}+v^{-2}\log Q(v)
 +\left(\frac{1+v}{1-v}\right)^2\log P(v)+\log(e_0/d)
\right].
\end{aligned}
\tag{17}
\]

Use exactly the scalar function from the five-arc proof,

\[
F(w)=\left(\frac{1+w}{1-w}\right)^2\log P(w)
 +w^{-2}\log Q(w)+\log\frac{2w}{1-w^2},\qquad
G(w)=F(w)+2\log(1-w).
\tag{18}
\]

Since

\[
\frac{f_0e_0}{d^2}
=\frac{2u}{1-u^2}\frac{2v}{1-v^2}
 \left(\frac{e_0}{d}\right)^2,
\]

the bracket in (17) is \(G(u)+G(v)-2\log d\). We have proved the
exact three-parameter cost identity

\[
\boxed{\displaystyle
\delta(u,v,d)=
\frac{2(d+2)-(d/2)\bigl(G(u)+G(v)-2\log d\bigr)}{4-d}.}
\tag{19}
\]

## 5. The parameter bound and the strict octagon comparison

Put

\[
w_* = \sqrt2-1,\qquad d_*=(2-\sqrt2)^2.
\]

Every parameter triple in (5) satisfies \(d<d_*\). Indeed, let
\(k=\sqrt{uv}\in(0,1)\). The elementary inequality \(u+v\ge2k\)
gives simultaneously

\[
e_0=1-u-v+uv\le(1-k)^2,\qquad
f_0=\frac{4uv}{1+u+v+uv}\le\frac{4k^2}{(1+k)^2}.
\tag{20}
\]

The first upper bound is strictly decreasing in \(k\); the second is
strictly increasing. They coincide exactly at \(k=w_*\), with common
value \(d_*\). For \(k\le w_*\) use the second bound, and for
\(k\ge w_*\) use the first. Thus

\[
\boxed{d<\min(e_0,f_0)\le d_*.}
\tag{21}
\]

The sharp envelope proved in [FIVE_ARC_PROGRESS.md, Section 5](FIVE_ARC_PROGRESS.md)
is

\[
G(w)\le M:=F(w_*)+\log d_*,\qquad
F(w_*)=(3+2\sqrt2)\log2,
\tag{22}
\]

with equality exactly at \(w=w_*\). That proof uses the strictly
concave function
\(x\mapsto -2\sqrt x\log(\sqrt x)/(1-\sqrt x)-\log(1+\sqrt x)\)
on \((0,1)\), applied to a pair of positive numbers whose squares sum
to one. Its domain includes every \(u,v\) in (5).

Applying (22) to (19) yields

\[
\delta(u,v,d)\ge
\mathcal B(d):=\frac{2(d+2)-d(M-\log d)}{4-d}.
\tag{23}
\]

For clarity, the needed monotonicity is also immediate by differentiation:

\[
\mathcal B'(d)
=\frac{4(4-M+\log d)-d}{(4-d)^2}<0
\qquad(0<d\le d_*).
\tag{24}
\]

Indeed \(4-M+\log d\le4-F(w_*)<0\). An exact check of the last
inequality is

\[
\log2>\frac{56}{81},\quad \sqrt2>\frac75
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
F(w_*)>\frac{29}{5}\frac{56}{81}=\frac{1624}{405}>4.
\]

The logarithm bound follows by integrating
\(2/(1-x^2)>2(1+x^2)\) over \(0<x<1/3\).
Combining (21), (23), and (24) gives the claimed strict comparison:

\[
\boxed{\displaystyle
\delta(u,v,d)>\mathcal B(d_*)
=\frac{8-4\sqrt2-\log2}{2\sqrt2-1}
=\delta_{\rm oct}.}
\tag{25}
\]

Multiplication by \(\sqrt{12}>0\) gives the corresponding actual cost
inequality. Every positive-duration 010202 competitor is therefore
excluded from global minimality by the concrete octagon competitor.

## 6. Sharp boundary and why the local sign test was insufficient

The lower bound is sharp as an infimum for this six-arc class. Take
\(u=v=w_*\) and \(0<d<d_*\). The factors are then

\[
(p,q,r,s,t,z)
=\left(\sqrt2,\sqrt2,\frac{d_*}{d},
\sqrt2,\sqrt2,\frac{d_*}{d}\right).
\tag{26}
\]

All entries in the state table are positive: the potentially restrictive
\(L_{w_*}\) decreases linearly in \(d\) and has the positive value
\(d_*\) at \(d=d_*\); the other entries are positive there and remain
positive for \(0<d<d_*\). The exact monotone vertex recurrence makes
these endpoint signs sufficient on each entire arc. Both endpoint
conditions follow from (2) and (8), as in WORD_HOLONOMY. Thus (26)
describes actual admissible trajectories, with cost
\(\mathcal B(d)\downarrow\delta_{\rm oct}\) as \(d\uparrow d_*\).
At the endpoint the third and sixth factors become one, their durations
vanish, and the remaining word is 0120 with all four factors \(\sqrt2\).
No positive six-arc member attains the infimum.

This proof also explains why the first proposed local obstruction did
not work. The two overlapping four-arc edge windows are 1010 across the
initial seam and 0202 in the last four arcs. In an edge omitting label
\(j\), the increasing half-plane coordinate has sign
\(p_{j+2}-p_{j+1}\). The necessary switch-straddling conditions from
[EDGE_SWITCH_BOUND.md, Section 6](EDGE_SWITCH_BOUND.md) become

\[
d<A_u,\qquad d/u>d,\qquad d<d/v,\qquad A_v>d.
\]

Every one of these inequalities holds automatically for \(0<u,v<1\).
The sign test therefore cannot itself rule out this word. The full
endpoint-derived cost identity and the global logarithmic envelope supply
the strict comparison that the sign conditions alone do not provide.

## 7. Position among the six-arc words

For context, there are four cyclically reduced six-arc orbits before
the exclusions above. This is a finite classification at six arcs only.
First merge adjacent equal labels. If the last label equals the first
label plus one modulo three, a twisted rebase merges across the seam
and reduces the description to at most five pieces, already compared in
[FIVE_ARC_PROGRESS.md](FIVE_ARC_PROGRESS.md).

Otherwise define six increments `ε_i∈{−1,+1}`, including the twisted
seam from `j_5` to `j_0+1`. Their sum is congruent to one modulo three
and is even, between `−6` and `6`. It is therefore `4` or `−2`.
There are either five positive increments or two. In the first case
rotation places the sole negative increment at the end. In the second,
the two indistinguishable positive positions have cyclic separation
one, two, or three, up to rotation. Starting the first control label
at zero gives the complete list:

| Representative | Increment pattern | Current conclusion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `012012` | `+++++−` | Every admissible member costs strictly more than the octagon, by SIX_ARC_012012 |
| `012102` | `++−−−−` | Comparison open |
| `010102` | `+−+−−−` | Infeasible: a twisted rebase gives `212121`, omitting vertex zero |
| `010202` | `+−−+−−` | Every admissible member costs strictly more than the octagon, by this note |

No reflection or symmetry assumption is used. Thus only three of the
four raw orbits can pass the existing cyclic ordering test. The `012012`
entry is the full cost comparison in [SIX_ARC_012012.md](SIX_ARC_012012.md).
It combines a compact feasible deformation with the actual endpoint-preserving
Hessian in [FORWARD_ALTERNATION_EXCLUSION.md](FORWARD_ALTERNATION_EXCLUSION.md),
added concurrently in commit `1b23521`. The compact deformation supplies the
global comparison that the local improvement alone does not imply.
An independent monotone deformation of the entire `010202` orbit appears in
[SIX_ARC_REVERSE_BLOCK.md](SIX_ARC_REVERSE_BLOCK.md), preserved from concurrent
commit `421eeb1` and independently reviewed. It also proves full feasibility
of the positive factor family. The sole remaining six-piece comparison is
`012102` (equivalently
`010210` after rebasing and relabeling).

## Remaining scope

The result excludes this entire twisted-rebasing orbit at six arcs.
The classification above does not compare its remaining open row. It supplies
no bound on arbitrary word length and no reduction of general measurable
controls to finitely many arcs. Those are separate mathematical problems.
