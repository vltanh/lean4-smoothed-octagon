\section*{Acknowledgments and the use of AI}

The mathematical argument of this paper was drafted by ChatGPT Pro (OpenAI), in the working
notes~\cite{Notes,LighterProofs}. Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic) wrote the Lean formalization, in
which several steps take shorter routes than the notes, and wrote this manuscript from the
formalization; the formalization of the book of Hales and Vajjha and of the maximum principle on
which it builds~\cite{SmoothedPolygonLean,OptimalControlLean,FixedPointLean} was written in the same
way. No person has checked the proof line by line. Its correctness rests on Lean's kernel, which
checks every step of the formal proof, and on the faithfulness of the Lean statements of
\cref{thm:main} to the mathematical ones, which \cref{app:formal-statements} describes.

We thank Thomas Hales and Koundinya Vajjha, whose formulation of the problem and whose book this
proof builds on.
